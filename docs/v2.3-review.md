# v2.3 firmware review (October 2026)

These notes come from reading `legacy/Tap-Express_v2.3-M5-Pro-Mini`, the last 2021 version, to work out what it actually does before rewriting it.

## What it does

| Control | Behaviour in v2.3 |
|---|---|
| Waveform rotary (pins 6/7/8) | 1 sine (JLed Breathe), 2 ramp-up saw (FadeOn), 3 square (Blink), 5 ramp-down saw (FadeOff), 6 random (JLed Candle), 7 constant ON. 4 does nothing. |
| Tempo pot (A0) | 60–2000 ms, smoothed over 10 readings. Takes over from tap tempo when moved by more than 20 ms. |
| Tap (pin 1) | Wired to the Line6 M5 tap switch. The period is the average of the last two intervals. |
| Multiply toggle (pins 3/4) | ×4 / ×0.5 / ×1 |
| Ramp toggle (pins 2/18) | Only the centre position is implemented: about 4 beats (`period × 4 / 255` ms per step) |
| Footswitch (pin 12, OneButton) | Click = latch, long press (500 ms) = momentary, double-click = shift |
| Shift | Red LED on pin 13. A click toggles the CD4066 lines (pins 16/17) to call up the M5's preset select. |
| EXP POT | Hardware only: a pot in parallel with the LDR sets the "off" resistance. There's no min/max intensity in firmware. |

### The ramp (constant ON only)
- **Hold:** after 500 ms it ramps 0→255. Releasing mid-ramp reverses it to 0. At the top it stays on while held. Releasing ramps it down, and pressing during the ramp-down reverses it back up.
- **Click:** it ramps up and latches at the top. The next click ramps down. Any press during a ramp reverses it.

## Issues found

1. **The ramp-length toggle is unfinished.** Cases 1 and 2 of `update_ramptime()` are empty.
2. **Ramp time is only computed when the ramp toggle changes.** At boot `period` is 2000, so the ramp is about 31 ms × 255 ≈ 8 s whatever the tempo.
3. **The tap is on pin 1 (TX) while `Serial` is active.** Every `Serial.print` drives the pin the tap switch is shorting.
4. **The tap interval can overflow.** `period` is a 16-bit `int` fed from `millis()` differences, and `MAXTAP` is never used. Tapping again after a long pause gives a garbage tempo.
5. **The multiply factor is inconsistent.** The tempo LED uses `period / 4` for ×4 while the effect uses `period × 4`. `MAXDELAY` also includes `factor`, so the pot range scales twice.
6. **The ramps block.** `delay()` sits inside `for` loops with a 300 ms pause at each direction change. Tap, pot, OneButton and the JLed LEDs all freeze during a ramp.
7. **The ramp is linear in PWM.** An LDR's resistance falls steeply at low light, so most of the sweep happens early.
8. **There are dead and duplicated bits.** `factorled` (pin 19) is never driven, `rampvalue` is shadowed, the same JLed setups appear twice (normal and LowActive), and some comments were copy-pasted from other pins.

v4.0 addresses all of these. See the CHANGELOG.
